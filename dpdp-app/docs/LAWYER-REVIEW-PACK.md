## What we are asking

VERIDIAN DPDP gives an organisation a list of jobs (a **firm** edition of 31 and an **institution** edition of 28) and, for each job, plain-language guidance a busy non-lawyer -- or an AI helping them -- follows. The pages say the list is not yet lawyer-reviewed. We would like counsel to read it and tell us, job by job, whether it is safe to put in front of customers.

Please look for four kinds of problem, in this order of seriousness:

1. **Wrong or overstated law** -- a section, rule, deadline or duty the text gets wrong, or states more firmly than the law does.
2. **A promise we cannot keep** -- wording that sounds like legal advice, a guarantee of compliance, or a substitute for the customer's own adviser.
3. **A missing duty or a missing caution** -- something a careful adviser would insist a small business or school be told.
4. **A job that should never be marked "doesn't apply"** -- or one where that answer needs more than a sentence of explanation.

The playbook text deliberately does **not** state section numbers, rule numbers, penalties or deadlines of its own; the law behind each job is looked up from the table in section 2 below, which is where a citation error would live.
