Become a Sales Partner

You sign in with your email. We send you a link. There is no password.

Who can be a Sales Partner

Anyone with a VERIDIAN sign-in. You do not need an organisation of your own. It is free to join.

How to join

  1. Sign in at /app/ with your email.
  2. Open Sales Partner. It is in the bar at the top of your page. If you have no organisation yet, it is on the "Open your organisation" screen.
  3. Read the Sales Partner terms and accept them. We record the version and the date.
  4. Add your UPI id or your bank details, so that we can pay you.
  • When both are done, you are an active partner and your personal link appears.
  • Your code stays the same every time. The link opens our public website. It holds your code and nothing else.

What happens when someone you referred pays

  1. A person opens an organisation in VERIDIAN with your code. We note that you referred them.
  2. The organisation pays us.
  3. We see the money and confirm the payment by hand. Until then, no commission is created.
  4. Your commission is created. It shows as waiting.
  • You earn 20% of each yearly payment, including every yearly renewal, for as long as that client stays.
  • Your own organisation, and any organisation you belong to, never counts. If you sign up through your own link, you earn nothing.
  • A sign-up through your link counts while your status is active.

How and when you are paid

A commission becomes payable 30 days after we confirm the client's payment.

We pay once a month, on the 10th, for everything that became payable before the end of the month before.

If your balance is under Rs 500, it carries forward to the next month.

We pay by UPI or bank transfer, to the details you gave us. We mark each commission paid after we send the money.

Tax (TDS) is deducted where the law requires it and shown on your statement.

These are the current terms. We may change them with 30 days' notice.

Your dashboard and your statement

  • You see how many organisations signed up, how many are in their free trial and how many pay. We show counts, never names.
  • You see what you have earned, what is waiting, what is payable and what was paid.
  • You get a statement by email on the 11th of each month. You can also download any month as a file. It shows gross, TDS and net.

What we never do

  • We never tell you the name of an organisation you referred.
  • We never show your payout details in full, and we never put them in an email.
  • We never create a commission before we have seen the client's money.
  • We never ask you to pay to join.

Please note

We cannot promise that you will earn anything. What you earn depends on whom you refer and whether they pay.

We do not give tax advice. For a question about a payout or about tax, write to us with the subject "Partner".

What VERIDIAN (VERy INDIAN) is

dpdp.veridian-aios.com is Indian software for running DPDP compliance, purpose-built for the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025, for CA, CS, audit and legal firms, their clients, and companies, institutions, schools and NGOs.

Who it is for:
  1. CA, CS, audit and legal firms · their clients
  2. Companies, institutions, schools and NGOs
  • It turns the law into a list of jobs, gives each job to the responsible person, and coordinates every stakeholder — owners, staff, vendors, group companies — through one email a week, with no accounts or passwords.
  • Each answer is recorded with a date and is added to the record, not edited, building the proof an organisation needs.
  • Every job is mapped to its legal source, including the SPDI Rules 2011 that apply until 13 May 2027.

It is not a law firm, does not certify (no DPDP certification exists in India), does not guarantee compliance, and does not keep your compliance documents (it keeps a fingerprint of each; payment proofs you upload are the one exception).
