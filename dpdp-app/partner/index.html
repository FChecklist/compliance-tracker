Become a Sales Partner

You sign in with your email. We send you a link. There is no password.

Who can be a Sales Partner

Anyone with a VERIDIAN account. Every person who belongs to an organisation in VERIDIAN has a personal link to share.

You do not have an organisation yet? Sign in with your email and open your own. It is free to start.

How you get your personal link

  1. Sign in at /app/ with your email.
  2. Open the page of your organisation.
  3. Press Share, in the bar at the very top of the page.
  4. Copy your link, or send it by WhatsApp or email.
  • The link opens our public website. It holds your personal code and nothing else.
  • Your code stays the same every time.

What happens when someone you referred pays

  1. A person opens an organisation in VERIDIAN with your code. We note that you referred them.
  2. The organisation pays us.
  3. We see the money and confirm the payment by hand. Until then, no commission is created.
  4. Your commission is created and shows as pending.
  • You earn 20% of each yearly payment, including every yearly renewal, for as long as that client stays.
  • Your own organisation never counts. If you sign up through your own link, you earn nothing.

How you are paid

We pay by hand, by UPI or bank transfer. Payment is not automatic, and this page does not fix a payout date.

Each commission is marked pending. We mark it paid after we send the money.

After your first referral you can see what you have earned, and what is still pending, in the Share box in the app.

Please note

We cannot promise that you will earn anything. What you earn depends on whom you refer and whether they pay.

This page does not cover tax on a commission. For a question about a payout or about tax, write to us with the subject "Partner".

What VERIDIAN (VERy INDIAN) is

dpdp.veridian-aios.com is Indian software for running DPDP compliance, purpose-built for the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025, for CA, CS, audit and legal firms, their clients, and companies, institutions, schools and NGOs.

Who it is for:
  1. CA, CS, audit and legal firms · their clients
  2. Companies, institutions, schools and NGOs
  • It turns the law into a list of jobs, gives each job to the responsible person, and coordinates every stakeholder — owners, staff, vendors, group companies — through one email a week, with no accounts or passwords.
  • Each answer is recorded with a date and cannot be edited, building the proof an organisation needs.
  • Every job is mapped to its legal source, including the SPDI Rules 2011 that apply until 13 May 2027.

It is not a law firm, does not certify (no DPDP certification exists in India), does not guarantee compliance, and never stores documents.
