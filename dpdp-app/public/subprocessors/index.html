Sub-processors

Version 1.1 · Effective 14 October 2026 · Read with the Privacy Notice

These are the outside services that handle personal data for us so the service can run. We use no others for customer data. If this list changes we change this page first, and we tell our customers by e-mail at least 14 days before a new service starts to handle their data. A customer who objects can write to us in that time.

ServiceWhat it doesWhere
SupabaseDatabase, sign-in and the small server functions that run the serviceMumbai, India (AWS ap-south-1)
CloudflareServes the website, network security, and routing of mail sent to dpdp@veridian-aios.comGlobal network
ResendSends our emails (sign-in codes, reminders, receipts) and receives mail sent to dpdp@veridian-aios.com. The content of those emails passes through itA US company. Our mail is handled in Tokyo, Japan (AWS ap-northeast-1)
RazorpayTakes card and UPI payments in its own payment window, through the Razorpay account of IT Services by Rajat Agarwal. It tells us a payment succeeded and gives its reference. We do not see or keep card numbersIndia
Central payment service of IT Services by Rajat AgarwalRecords each payment (its reference, amount, date and method) so our books match Razorpay. It is run by us on Cloudflare and keeps no card numbersCloudflare's global network
GitHubKeeps our software and runs our scheduled jobs. A private copy of the payment records of the central payment service is also kept thereUSA
Google (mailbox)Our operator's mailbox that receives forwarded copies of mail sent to dpdp@veridian-aios.comGoogle's network

An AI assistant you choose yourself (for example one you give an AI work link to) is not our sub-processor. It acts for you, under its own terms.

Questions or objections: dpdp@veridian-aios.com (subject: Data request).
