Pricing

Version 1.0 · Effective 1 October 2026 · Read together with the Terms of Service.

The short version. Some features are free. Paid features are quoted to you in writing before you pay anything. Prices are in Indian rupees (INR) and exclusive of GST. Every payment gets a GST invoice.

1. What is free

Some features of VERIDIAN DPDP are free. We say so on the page where a free feature appears. Until a fee is agreed in writing you owe nothing (Terms of Service, clause 7).

2. What is paid, and how you are told the price

Fees for other features are quoted in writing: a proposal, an order form or an invoice. The quote states what is included, the amount in INR, the period it covers and any tax. We do not charge anything that was not agreed in writing.

3. Tax

All fees are in Indian rupees and are exclusive of GST and other taxes, which you pay in addition at the rate that applies. We issue a GST invoice for every payment, in the name and GSTIN (if any) you give us.

4. How to pay

You can pay online by UPI, debit or credit card, net banking or wallet through our payment partner Razorpay, using a payment link made for you when you press Pay online in the app, or by bank transfer against our invoice. We never see or keep your card number on this Service. Invoices are payable within the time stated on them; if none, within 15 days.

5. Changes to fees

We may change fees for a later period on 30 days' written notice. You may end the Service before the change takes effect (Terms of Service, clause 7).
