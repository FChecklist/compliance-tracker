Delivery Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective 1 October 2026 · Read together with the Terms of Service.

The short version. VERIDIAN DPDP is online software from a software company. We deliver no physical products or services; everything is digital, over the internet. Nothing physical is shipped and there are no delivery charges.

1. What you receive

Access to the Service at veridian-aios.com: a sign-in for you and for the people your organisation invites, and the editions and features agreed in writing.

2. When

We set up access and send the sign-in details to the email address on your order, normally within one working day after we have your order or payment confirmation. For a larger set-up we tell you the expected date in the proposal.

3. Charges

There is no shipping or delivery charge. The only amounts are the fees shown or agreed in writing (see Pricing); they are the whole amount payable, and GST is not charged.

4. If access does not arrive

Write to dpdp@veridian-aios.com with the subject "Access", with your organisation name and order or invoice number. We will fix it or tell you what is holding it up.
