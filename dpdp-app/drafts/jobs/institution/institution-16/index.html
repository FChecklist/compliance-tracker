Why it matters

Reasonable security is the duty most likely to be tested, because a leak is the moment everyone looks. Passwords, access limits, backups and a log of who opened what are what 'reasonable' looks like for a small organisation.

The Aadhaar Act 2016 restricts how Aadhaar numbers may be stored, shared and displayed; an unmasked Aadhaar copy is among the records most often leaked.

From 13 May 2027, a person can take an unresolved grievance to the Data Protection Board of India, which can inquire and impose the penalties the Act's Schedule sets. This page does not quote figures — the Board decides each case, and no penalty order has been issued in India to date.
