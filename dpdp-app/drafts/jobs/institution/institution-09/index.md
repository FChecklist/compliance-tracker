## The law, by section and rule

- Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, section 4 — grounds for processing personal data — only for a lawful purpose, with consent or under a listed legitimate use.
- Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, section 8(7) — erase personal data when consent is withdrawn or the purpose is no longer served, and make the Data Processor erase it too, unless a law requires retention.

## Why it matters

You cannot protect, explain or delete what you have not written down. The map of where personal data sits — which folder, which software, which cupboard — is the base that every notice, consent, security step and deletion request is measured against.

From 13 May 2027, a person can take an unresolved grievance to the Data Protection Board of India, which can inquire and impose the penalties the Act's Schedule sets. This page does not quote figures — the Board decides each case, and no penalty order has been issued in India to date.
