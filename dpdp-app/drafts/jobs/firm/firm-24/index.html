DPDP job firm-24 · Part 5: Firms you share data with · for companies, firms and NGOs

Group company signs a data-sharing agreement

Group company signs a data-sharing agreement. For a company, firm or NGO, this comes from DPDP Act 2023 §8(2) and SPDI Rules 2011 rule 7 — the SPDI part is in force TODAY, until 13 May 2027, and the DPDP part is in force from 13 May 2027. Usually done by Group company — a sister or group company you share data with — legally a separate company; it counts as done when the person responsible presses Yes and the date and name are logged.

The law, by section and rule

  • Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, section 8(2) — a Data Processor may be engaged only under a valid contract.
  • Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, rule 7 — transfer of information only to a party that ensures the same level of protection, and only where necessary for a contract or with consent.

Both apply: the today's-law duty is what you can be held to now; the DPDP duty is what replaces it on 13 May 2027. Doing it once satisfies both.

In force today, or from 13 May 2027?

  • DPDP Act 2023 and Rules 2025 — in force from 13 May 2027
  • IT Act §43A and SPDI Rules 2011 — in force TODAY, until 13 May 2027

Do this now: the one page counts this job as required by today's law, and it is late the day it is missed — not on 13 May 2027.

Who usually does it

Group company — a sister or group company you share data with — legally a separate company.

This job is answered by an outside firm — a Data Processor in the Act's words — not by your own staff. It is free for them; the data agreement is what makes them answer, and they are blocked until they sign.

If you have no group company, the job can be marked "doesn't apply" and it drops out of the file.

What proof looks like

A dated declaration in the VERIDIAN record: the person responsible presses Yes, and the date and their name go into a log that cannot be edited afterwards. VERIDIAN does not connect to your systems or read your files — it records what you declare and holds you to it.

Proof kind: declaration. Proof mode: declare. VERIDIAN gives this job 12 days from the day the file starts; it is done once, not repeated — unless the answers change.

Why it matters

The organisation stays answerable for what its website firm, payroll firm or software vendor does with the data. A signed data agreement is the only way to make an outside firm answer — and the only way to prove you asked.

From 13 May 2027, a person can take an unresolved grievance to the Data Protection Board of India, which can inquire and impose the penalties the Act's Schedule sets. This page does not quote figures — the Board decides each case, and no penalty order has been issued in India to date.

This is today's law: under section 43A of the Information Technology Act, 2000, a body corporate that is negligent in keeping reasonable security practices can be ordered to compensate the person harmed.
