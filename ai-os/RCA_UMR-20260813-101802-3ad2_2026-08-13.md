## Root cause

`UMR-20260813-101802-3ad2`'s `killed` status is a **stale snapshot of invocation 1's outcome
only**. `scan_stuck_tasks`'s SIGKILL write is never reconciled against a later successful resume
under the same `task_dir`/service unit — the dispatch-tracking row is written once, at the moment
of the kill, and nothing subsequently re-examines it once the auto-restarted worker goes on to
finish real work. This is the same class of gap already flagged and left open in
[[veridian-umr-0faf-killed-rca-real-work-mislabeled]],
[[veridian-umr-f9a4-killed-rca-real-work-mislabeled]], and
[[veridian-umr-f13c-killed-rca-real-work-already-done-plus-hard-stop-restart-gap]]: systemd keeps
auto-resuming hard-stopped/blocked tasks, but the UMR tracking layer has no corresponding
reconciliation step, so a genuinely completed task can sit permanently mislabeled `killed` unless
a follow-on RCA happens to catch it.

kill time before treating `killed` as final.
