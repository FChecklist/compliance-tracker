# Customer data processing agreement (draft text for the lawyer)

DRAFT. Intended to sit under the Terms of Service clause "Processor terms". Plain words on purpose. Square brackets need a decision.

**1. Roles.** The customer is the Data Fiduciary for the personal data it records in VERIDIAN DPDP ("Customer Data"). SHOBHA KAMAL SOLUTIONS PRIVATE LIMITED ("we") is its Data Processor.

**2. Instructions.** We process Customer Data only to provide the service and as the customer instructs through the product. If we think an instruction breaks the law, we say so.

**3. People.** Only people who need it can reach Customer Data, and they are bound to confidentiality.

**4. Security.** We keep reasonable safeguards: encrypted connections, each customer's data separated in the database by row-level security, role-based access for our staff, a dated record of changes that is added to and not edited, payout details encrypted, links that expire.

**5. Sub-processors.** We use the sub-processors on https://veridian-aios.com/subprocessors/. We change that page before we add or replace one, and tell the customer [30] days ahead. The customer may object within [14] days; if we cannot meet the objection the customer may end the service and take the refund the Refund Policy allows.

**6. Where.** The database is in Mumbai. E-mail passes through Resend, handled in Tokyo. Transfers outside India follow section 16 of the Act.

**7. Breach.** We tell the customer's owner within 24 hours of becoming aware of a breach of Customer Data, with what we know, and keep them updated. We help the customer with its own notices to the Board and to people.

**8. People's rights.** We give the customer the tools in the product to answer access, correction, erasure and withdrawal requests, and we help where a tool is not enough. A request that reaches us for the customer's data goes to the customer.

**9. Return and deletion.** When the service ends we make Customer Data available for export for 30 days, then delete it. We keep only what the law requires us to keep; the dated record of changes may be kept for [period].

**10. Audit.** We answer reasonable written questions once a year and give a summary of our measures. On-site audits only where the law requires [or on reasonable notice and at the customer's cost].

**11. AI.** We do not use Customer Data to train AI models. If the customer's user gives an AI work link to an AI assistant, that assistant acts for the user under its own terms and is not our sub-processor.

**12. Liability.** As in the Terms of Service [lawyer to confirm this clause is consistent].
