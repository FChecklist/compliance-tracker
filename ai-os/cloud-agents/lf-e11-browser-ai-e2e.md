Read `lf-e9-COMMON.md` and `PREAMBLE.md`. Branch `claude/lf-e11-browser-ai-e2e` from `origin/main`.

## Requirements (owner)
R11 the person's browser AI (Chrome, Edge, Safari...) finds and uses PROJEXA automatically; R6 an AI can work on the person's data; R7 an AI can create, edit and delete AS PER THE PERSON'S ROLE (deletes become drafts the person confirms unless the person's own "act without asking" switch is on); R5 an AI can NEVER change the software; the person's role and organisation isolation bind the AI exactly as they bind the person. PROJEXA's own in-app AI is OFF (zero model cost; `PROJEXA_INTERNAL_AI_ENABLED` unset). Spec: `docs/local-first/BROWSER_AI.md`; code `src/lib/local-first/ai/*` (api, attach, AiAttach, tools, webmcp, manual, drafts, integrity, immutability, registry, `function-registry.json`), `/llms.txt`, `/ai-manual.json`, `outbox-drafts.ts`, `AiDraftConfirm`.
