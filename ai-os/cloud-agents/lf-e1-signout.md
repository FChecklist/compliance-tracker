# Package lf-e1-signout (laptop app, repo projexa)

Branch `claude/lf-e1-signout` from `origin/feat/local-first-complete`. Read `ai-os/cloud-agents/lf-e-COMMON.md` (compliance-tracker clone, branch `origin/feat/lf-sync-backend`) first for the context (skip the module-conversion rules).

## Goal (requirements R9 "logged in forever until the person chooses to log out" and R10 "the app is not deleted until the person chooses")
The merged integration branch has TWO halves that were built separately and never joined:
- `src/lib/local-first/identity.ts` (PWA package): a durable identity mirror. An UNEXPECTED sign-out (cookie vanished, another tab, a failed refresh while offline) is not believed: the session is rebuilt from the mirror (`restoreSessionIfMissing`). `signOutDeliberately(deps)` is "THE sign-out": it clears the mirror, tells the service worker to delete the person's release caches, and ends the Supabase session (by hand when offline). **It is called from NO sign-out path.** Consequence today: a person who clicks Sign out can be silently signed back in from the mirror. That breaks the order "logged in until the person logs out".
- `src/lib/local-first/sign-out.ts` (client-core package): `finishLocalWorkspaceOnSignOut()` sends the outbox while the session is alive and deletes this laptop's copy of the workspace when nothing is pending (or keeps it and says so).
There are FOUR sign-out paths: `src/components/shell/AccountMenu.tsx`, `src/components/AppTopbar.tsx`, `src/components/SettingsClient.tsx`, and `src/components/shell/M24Shell.tsx` (its SIGNED_OUT reaction). `src/components/sign-out-local-first.test.tsx` guards that every path calls the workspace step (some checks are structural).
