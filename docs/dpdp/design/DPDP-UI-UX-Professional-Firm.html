VERIDIAN DPDP · Design A: the Professional Firm

For CA, CS, legal and audit firms that run DPDP for many client companies. The firm's daily question is “which of my clients needs me today?”, so the design starts from the portfolio, not from one organisation.

Design drawings only. Every screen below is a mock-up with made-up firm, clients and numbers. It is not the live app and nothing here was built or connected to the database. A green tag means the capability exists in the product today; an amber tag means it is proposed.
What makes this design different from the organisation design
  • Navigation: a left sidebar by practice task (Portfolio, Clients, Weekly board, Reminders, Reports, Team, Billing, Branding) and a client switcher at the top.
  • Layout: dense tables, filters and bulk actions; many clients at once.
  • Vocabulary: clients, engagements, partner, manager, associate, sign-off, retainer.
  • Weekly cycle: done per client, but seen and signed across clients on the weekly board.
  • The firm's own brand on what clients see.
