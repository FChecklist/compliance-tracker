VERIDIAN DPDP · Design B: the Organisation

For a school, university, hospital or company that holds the personal data of its own people: students, parents, staff, alumni. A small internal team does the weekly routine, so the design speaks in everyday words and guides one step at a time.

Design drawings only. Every screen below is a mock-up with a made-up school and made-up numbers. It is not the live app and nothing here was built or connected to the database. A green tag means the capability exists in the product today (the layout and words here are new); an amber tag means it is proposed.
What makes this design different from the firm design
  • Navigation: a simple top bar in everyday words (Home, This week, What we hold, Parents & minors, Notices, Requests, If something goes wrong, People).
  • Layout: big friendly cards and one question at a time; no tables of many clients.
  • Vocabulary: “what we hold”, “sort it”, “keep the proof”, “parents”, “principal”; no legal terms in the way.
  • One organisation with its campuses and departments, not a list of clients.
  • Children first: parent or guardian consent, notices for minors, requests from parents.
