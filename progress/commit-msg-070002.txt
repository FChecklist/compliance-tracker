Split task-execution-engine.ts by responsibility

AI Engineering Quality gap-closure (Medium finding: monolithic files
and low test coverage). task-execution-engine.ts (2437 lines) combined
3 distinct responsibilities in one file. Splits into:
- src/lib/task-execution/dispatch-tools.ts: dispatchTool(), byte-identical
  extraction, re-exported from the original path so existing call sites
  (projexa assistant route, fde-service) keep working unchanged.
- src/lib/task-execution/dispatch-engine.ts: dispatchEngine() plus helpers
  (truthy, parseNumberList), byte-identical extraction. Adds
  dispatch-engine.test.ts (13 tests) covering the previously-untested
  pure helpers.
- task-execution-engine.ts itself: down to executeTask() plus
  orchestration helpers, a single coherent responsibility.

schema.ts (the other half of this finding) is deliberately not split in
this change -- see progress notes for the risk-based reasoning
(Drizzle relations() cross-table coupling makes it non-mechanical,
unlike this extraction).
